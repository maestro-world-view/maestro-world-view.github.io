AI resume builder FAQs

What is an AI resume builder?

An AI resume builder is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to help you create and refine your resume. These tools often have features like customizable templates, content suggestions, job description optimization, feedback, grammar checks, and revision support.

The teams behind resume builder tools like Enhancv have created tailored templates and prompts that produce polished resume content. That's the biggest advantage of specialized AI resume builder tools over out-of-the-box AI programs, such as ChatGPT.

Enhancv offers a range of AI features to help you create your resume with ease:

  • Templates and customization: Designed to be both visually appealing and to meet the standards expected in different industries.
  • AI-powered resume content generation, creative suggestions, or custom requests: Enhancv's AI Assistant suggests content based on your job title, job description, or your custom prompts.
  • Job description tailoring: Our AI Resume Builder suggests keywords and phrases that are likely to be favorable in these systems based on your job title. Plus, it does so much more, such as generating sections and writing a whole cover letter.
  • Feedback and revision suggestions: We've built a resume checker that provides clear feedback and a simple grading system on your resume's overall quality. It also provides a checklist with suggestions for improving layout, grammar, and wording.
Is Enhancv ATS-friendly?

Different ATS systems flag certain formatting issues that can lead to parsing errors, which can make some resume templates less ATS-friendly. Here's how our builder addresses each of these points, and why we're confident in our ATS-readiness.

  • Enhancv resumes don't include graphics, photos, or word art that aren't ATS-Friendly: Our templates use decorative icons and images as visual elements only, never as text containers. All text remains selectable and lives in the text layer. That means parsers simply skip the iconography without losing any data.
  • Enhancv resumes aren't uploaded as an image (flattened PDFs): We export proper PDFs with a full text layer, not rasterized images. You can copy-paste any text from our resumes, exactly what parsers read. Flattened PDFs (a known parsing failure) don't occur in our generation pipeline.
  • Enhancv resumes don't use complex designs with tables, headers, and footers: We use consistent section grouping and rely on HTML-to-PDF text flow, not embedded Word tables or footers. Only one legacy template uses a minimal table structure, and even that's fully textual, not graphic. Contact info sits in the main body, not headers/footers, so it's accessible to parsers.
  • Enhancv resumes use a columned layout: Our column structure keeps each section separate, for example, Experience on the left and Skills or Education on the right. We never split a single section across both columns, so the reading order (left to right, top to bottom) stays coherent.
  • Enhancv resumes have clear sections and consistent formatting: Each section in Enhancv templates is clearly labeled ("Experience," "Education," "Skills") and uses a consistent heading style. These titles match parser dictionaries used by Sovren and RChilli, improving section classification.

Every major ATS risk category is either resolved by design or limited to user input, not the builder's output. Our PDFs meet modern parsing standards, including selectable text, consistent structure, clear labeling, and no flattened layers.

Because of this, Enhancv resumes are ATS-compatible by design rather than through workarounds. Calling our templates "not ATS-friendly" or suggesting caution would be inaccurate.

How do I use AI to write my resume?

You can use the AI of your choice (be it ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, xAI, etc.) to assist you in writing your resume, rather than outsourcing this task fully to AI.

Here are some resume-related tasks that AI can greatly help you with:

  • Starting a framework: Ask AI to provide a resume format tailored to your industry or role.
  • Be precise with prompts: Instead of general requests like "Write about my work experience," go for "Highlight my achievements as a marketing manager."
  • Using action words: Request AI to suggest impactful verbs that match your responsibilities.
  • Adapting for the job: Customize each resume for the role, focusing on keywords from the job description.
  • Aligning your skills: Match your skill set to the requirements listed in the job posting.
  • Adding measurable results: Include numbers, percentages, or other metrics to showcase your accomplishments.
  • Keeping it brief: Ask AI to rephrase lengthy sections into concise, high-impact statements.
  • Reviewing for errors: Have AI proofread for grammar, clarity, and typos.
  • Writing a standout summary: Use AI to craft a professional summary that highlights your strengths and key achievements.
  • Polishing the final draft: After generating the content, ask AI for a final review to ensure a sleek and professional finish.
What's the best resume template to pass ATS?

You don't need to pass anything with applicant tracking systems. We ran a test study that compared how Word/Google Docs resumes are parsed compared to Enhancv templates. The reality is that ATS systems don't have an issue with colors, backgrounds, or icons.

In fact, ATS systems look for the name of a section and then transfer that information to the ATS. But the recruiter looks at the entire resume.

Our newest study on auto-rejection confirms that no automated rejection is happening. Recruiters primarily filter by skills and experience match. The less aligned your resume is, the lower it appears in their sorted view.

So, to answer the question: pick the template you like best. All Enhancv templates parse at 90% or higher. Simple templates work well for traditional fields, while two-column templates let you fit more information on a single page. Choose based on what you need.

Is Enhancv a design tool like Canva?

No. Enhancv is an AI resume builder with content feedback, not a design tool. We focus on what your resume says, not only on how it looks.

  • AI rewrites your bullets based on the job you're applying to. Canva's resume templates fill in text but don't rewrite it.
  • Recruiter-style review flags vague phrases, missing keywords, and weak verbs. We analyze your content, not just your layout.
  • Match score compares your resume against a specific job description and shows you where it aligns and where it falls short. That comparison doesn't exist in a design product.
  • ATS-tested templates parse at 90%+ by Sovren and RChilli. Design-tool templates often use images and decorative elements that parsers skip over.

Canva and similar design tools are great for visual work. For a resume that gets past applicant tracking systems and reaches a recruiter, you need the content layer, not just the layout.

Can recruiters tell if my resume was written by AI?

As long as your application is customized with your job history and accomplishments, it's unlikely that recruiters can tell if you used AI. AI can help improve your resume writing, but lying on a resume is unacceptable.

Are resumes scanned by AI?

Resumes in some companies are scanned by an applicant tracking system (ATS), which may or may not have AI features. These systems parse resumes into sections and match them with keywords from the job description. However, contrary to popular belief, very few companies actually use ATS to filter or automatically reject applicants.
