Professional Résumé Writing for Clients Australia Wide

1300 Resume supports clients across every state and territory, delivering professionally written résumés, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, selection criteria responses, together with career coaching and interview preparation for clients at every career level. Whether you are based in Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra, Hobart, Darwin or regional Australia, our services are delivered seamlessly via phone, email and video consultation.

Led by Monique Thompson, a senior résumé writer and career coach with 30 years’ experience, 1300 Resume provides tailored career documents for government, executive, professional, corporate, trade, mining, oil and gas, healthcare and specialist industry roles. Government and executive applications are personally written by Monique, while industry-specific résumés are prepared by experienced writers who understand the language, expectations and recruitment standards of each sector.

Every document is strategically written to highlight your strengths, achievements, experience and future potential, giving you a stronger foundation for applications, promotions and career progression. To discuss your requirements, please contact us by phone or request a quote, and we will respond as soon as possible.
