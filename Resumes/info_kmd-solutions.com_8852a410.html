NDIS Resume Writing in Sydney

We specialise in NDIS resume writing services in Sydney, supporting participants to create professional resumes aligned with their employment goals and NDIS plans. Whether you are entering the workforce, returning to work, or exploring new opportunities, we provide structured, supportive guidance every step of the way.

Our service is suitable for participants with psychosocial disability, autism, learning differences, physical disability, and cognitive challenges.

How We Help NDIS Participants

  • Build confidence through supportive, one-on-one guidance
  • Identify strengths, transferable skills, and capabilities
  • Create clear, professional resumes aligned with job goals
  • Present lived experience positively and appropriately
  • Prepare documents for job applications and employment pathways

We take a strengths-based approach, ensuring each resume reflects the participant’s abilities in a way that feels authentic, empowering, and employer-ready.

Resume Support for NDIS Job Readiness

Our NDIS resume services are designed to support job readiness and employment outcomes. We help participants prepare for:

  • Job applications and interviews
  • Referrals to Disability Employment Services (DES)
  • Work experience or volunteering opportunities
  • Transitioning into open employment

We focus on practical outcomes—helping participants feel more prepared, capable, and confident in their job search.

Face-to-Face NDIS Resume Services in Sydney

We offer in-person resume writing support across Sydney, including Parramatta, Blacktown, Penrith, North Sydney, and the Inner West. Sessions are also available online via Zoom or Teams for participants across Australia.

  • Calm, supportive environment

Our NDIS Resume Writing Process

1. Free Consultation

We speak with participants, Support Coordinators or Plan Managers to understand goals, needs, and suitability.

2. Personalised Session

We explore work history, strengths, interests, and employment goals in a supportive and structured way.

3. Resume Creation

We develop a professional resume that highlights strengths, skills, and experience while aligning with employer expectations.

4. Review and Finalisation

We refine the resume to ensure it feels accurate, comfortable, and empowering for the participant.
