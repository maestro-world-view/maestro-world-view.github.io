    About ResumesCanada

    ResumesCanada is your trusted partner in job preparation and career advancement. Our team delivers strategic, results-driven services that provide an effective Return on Investment (ROI). We assist clients from Canada and around the globe in crafting top-tier job application documents.

    Whether you are looking to land your first job, secure your next role, or achieve your dream career, ResumesCanada has a comprehensive suite of services tailored to your needs. We work with clients in various industries, including government, sales, technology, telecommunications, health, and finance.

    Meet Our Lead Consultant: Josh Denomey

    Josh Denomey, BA, HRM, and CHRP and CSC holder, is the owner and lead consultant at ResumesCanada. With over 15 years of experience in career counselling, business, and human resources, Josh has guided clients across diverse industries, such as logistics, technology, finance, accounting, engineering, insurance, production, and numerous others. His dedication and expertise make him a sought-after leader in resume writing and career strategy.

    Our offerings include creating professional resumes and cover letters, enhancing personal branding through LinkedIn, and developing specialized tools like marketing briefs for those who need concise, impactful documents. Clients trust Josh and his team for comprehensive business and career advice that sets them apart in the job market.
