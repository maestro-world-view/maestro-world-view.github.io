Part-Time MBA Request a Resume Review

The Evening MBA and Weekend MBA Programs at Chicago Booth bring together high-achieving professionals from a wide range of backgrounds and industries.

While their interests and goals may vary, our students share a relentless curiosity, a commitment to collaboration, and a willingness to challenge the status quo.

We’re happy to help you determine if either of our Part-Time MBA Programs are a good fit for you. Simply upload your one- to two-page MBA resume, and we’ll contact you within seven business days to talk about your next steps. We look forward to hearing from you!
